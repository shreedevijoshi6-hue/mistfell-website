Welcome to MistFell

Nature holds an extraordinary library of biological knowledge. For generations, traditional knowledge systems have recognised the importance of medicinal plants, roots, herbs, fruits and natural compounds.

MistFell aims to connect this knowledge with modern scientific research, biotechnology and molecular understanding.

We are building a knowledge-driven platform to investigate, organise and communicate medicinal intelligence through scientific literature and research.

Founder • Shreedevi U Joshi

Shreedevi U Joshi

Founder, MistFell

MistFell began with a curiosity about the relationship between nature, traditional medicinal knowledge and the human body.

Across generations, people have observed and documented the medicinal importance of plants, herbs, roots and fruits. Yet much of this knowledge deserves deeper scientific exploration and wider accessibility.

Shreedevi's vision is to build a knowledge-driven platform that brings together ancient medicinal wisdom and modern scientific understanding.

Through MistFell, she aims to encourage curiosity, scientific literacy and research into plant compounds, biotechnology and biological mechanisms.

Her belief is simple: knowledge should not remain limited to a few. Understanding nature and biological science should become more accessible to everyone.
